# Taste

## Visual effects & animation

- Prefers restrained animation polish over flashy effects — explicitly rejected "more flashing" when asking for a cooler banking animation; wants subtle, cohesive motion, not added gimmicks. Confidence: 0.8
- Prefers companion/formation entities (e.g. drones) to animate as part of the whole unit's animation — the entire formation should bank/swing together around the ship's pivot, rather than each entity doing an independent effect. Confidence: 0.7
- Wants companion entities (drones) to look like mini versions of the player's own ship — same loadout hull sprite and tint, facing forward — rather than generic sprites rotated sideways to act as turrets. Confidence: 0.8
- Prefers enemy projectiles to look polished rather than crude. Confidence: 0.6
