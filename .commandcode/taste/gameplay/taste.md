# Taste

## Game design

- Wants wingman drones positioned at the ship's sides and behind it, providing supplementary fire in the direction they sit (left/right from the flanks, down from the rear) — flank/rear coverage rather than forward fire. Confidence: 0.8
- Prefers enemy encounters driven by varied flight/movement choreography (spirals, weaves, arcs, etc.) over high bullet volume — explicitly wants fewer bullets and more unique flying patterns, with fun coming from patterns/choreography rather than bullet density alone. Confidence: 0.9
- Prefers bosses to have distinct, specialized attack identities with telegraphed, learnable counterplay rather than interchangeable high-volume bullet spam — each boss should be uniquely identifiable and avoidable through learning. Confidence: 0.9
- Dislikes excessive projectile curve/boomerang behavior where bullets arc back toward the boss — prefers clean, readable projectile trajectories for boss attacks. Confidence: 0.85
- Prefers enemy ships to ALWAYS face the direction they are flying (velocity-aligned orientation) with smooth, gradual banked turns — explicitly demanded no sudden sharp angle turns and circular/looping flight paths ("fly in circles and loops!!!!!!"); this supersedes the earlier preference for tipping-only banking with the nose kept forward. Confidence: 0.9
- Prefers enemy ships to fly faster with more urgent, high-tempo passes rather than slower drifting movement. Confidence: 0.85
- Prefers predictable, readable large-scale flight patterns over erratic/jittery choreography — explicitly wants side-entry → big circular loop → exit in another direction, with large legible geometric maneuvers that are telegraphed and easy to read. Confidence: 0.9
- Prefers stage hazards to be telegraphed/readable rather than striking without warning — asked what the "beam of light that suddenly appears and damages my ship" was, indicating surprise/unidentifiable hazards are a confusion point; explicitly requested a preview window before the solar flare deals damage. Confidence: 0.85
- Wants side-entering enemies to be clearly visible and perform their flight patterns center-screen: they should spawn fully off-screen at the sides at a visible mid-height, fly in across the screen, do their loops/sweeps in the middle of the playfield, then exit — not clip along the screen edges where they're barely visible. Confidence: 0.9
- Wants ALL projectiles (player, enemy, boss, and stage-gimmick shots) to travel in perfectly straight lines — explicitly "I don't ever want bullets swaying back and forth... straight line always"; wants sway (wave amplitude/frequency) and curve/arc mechanics removed entirely, not just reduced. Confidence: 0.95
